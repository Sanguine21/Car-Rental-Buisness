About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Madhubani

We are a trusted local car rental service based in Laheriyaganj, Madhubani, Bihar. With over 10 years of experience, we understand the needs of our customers. Whether it's a wedding, a family trip, an event, or airport transfer, we provide a car with a driver and ensure a comfortable and safe journey — no fixed packages, just the ride you need.

We serve all over Bihar including Patna, Gaya, and Darbhanga, and also take you to neighbouring states like Jharkhand (Deoghar) and West Bengal (Kolkata) whenever you need to travel. Our fleet is well-maintained and drivers are professional and local.
